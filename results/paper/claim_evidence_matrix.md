# Claim-to-Evidence Matrix

## Overview

This matrix establishes formal evidentiary grounding for every scientific claim considered for inclusion in the AgentGuard research paper. In accordance with Phase 20 scientific integrity standards:
- Claims supported by statistically significant test-set evidence are marked **Supported** or **Strongly Supported**.
- Claims where empirical evidence is ambiguous, underpowered, or lacks statistical significance ($p > 0.05$) are explicitly designated **Inconclusive** or **Not Supported**.
- Speculative claims and assertions implying physical causality are marked **Rejected / Not Claimed**.

## 2. Policy for Drafting the Research Paper

1. **Claims 1, 3, 5, 6, and 7** are adopted as the core empirical contributions of the paper.
2. **Claim 2 is explicitly rejected:** The paper explicitly notes that on the standardized benchmark test population, classical ML baselines perform on par with or exceed the Temporal GNN, and the bootstrap difference does not achieve statistical significance ($p = 0.080$).
3. **Claim 4 is presented with scientific transparency:** The lack of statistically significant test-set delta-F1 across ablation variants on the $N=35$ sample size is reported without modification.
4. **Claim 8 is explicitly guarded:** The paper includes a dedicated mandate stating that all attributions reflect model-internal sensitivity, not physical causality.
